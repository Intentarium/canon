# Accountability without authority is organizational debt

**Maturity:** Hypothesis

## Problem

A person or team owns an outcome but lacks the decision rights, access, or resources required to produce it.

## Pattern

Align accountability with explicit authority, bounded execution rights, evidence requirements, and a defined escalation path.

## Failure modes

- Escalation is informal or punitive.
- Decision rights are implied rather than written.
- Heroics hide structural failure.

## Evidence needed

Repeated field reports showing reduced delay, rework, and dependency friction.
