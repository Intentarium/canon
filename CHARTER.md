# Intentarium Charter

Intentarium is a public institution for making human intent durable in systems people and agents can trust.

Its thesis is **autonomy without chaos**: people and intelligent systems gain useful agency when intent, authority, constraints, trust, evidence, escalation, and feedback are explicit.

## Purpose

We convert practical experience into portable patterns, specifications, examples, and tools. The public canon is useful to humans and machines, independently inspectable, and never dependent on one model provider.

## Commitments

- Preserve human intent through automation.
- Distinguish hypotheses from field-tested patterns.
- Make provenance, uncertainty, approval, and revision visible.
- Prefer useful boundaries over opaque control.
- Treat decisions as durable artifacts rather than ephemeral conversations.
- Keep the canon portable, inspectable, and public.
- Publish only deliberately promoted, public-safe knowledge.

## Authority

Agents and contributors may research, test, critique, and propose. They may not alter canonical memory, promote pattern maturity, or publish material without human authorization through the repository's review process.

## Exclusions

Intentarium does not impersonate individuals, expose raw private context, treat generated text as established knowledge, or grant a runtime authority over its own governing controls.
