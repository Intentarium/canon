# Intentarium Charter

Intentarium is a public institution for making human intent durable in systems people and agents can trust.

We publish patterns, evidence, decisions, and tools that make authority, boundaries, trust, escalation, and feedback explicit. We do not publish private context, impersonate people, or treat unreviewed generation as established knowledge.

## Commitments

- Preserve human intent through automation.
- Distinguish hypotheses from field-tested patterns.
- Make provenance and revision visible.
- Prefer useful boundaries over opaque control.
- Keep the canon portable, inspectable, and public.
